Session 04 / October 12th, 1994

The Whispering Sewers

"The darkness here isn't just an absence of light; it's a presence that breathes, smells of copper and damp earth."

Narrative Beats

We met Julian Thorne at the broken junction. He looked paler than usual, his eyes darting toward the shadows of the ventilation shaft. He spoke of a "Breach" in the Masquerade that started three levels down.

The smell of stagnant blood was overwhelming near the St. Jude's Outflow. We found the ritual marks—charred bone and geometric patterns that shouldn't exist in this century.
